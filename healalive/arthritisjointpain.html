Common Causes of Joint Pain and Why You Shouldn’t Ignore Them

Joint pain can stem from many underlying conditions. Without proper diagnosis, it’s easy to treat the wrong issue or overlook a more serious problem.

Common illnesses and causes of joint pain

  • Osteoarthritis: Cartilage wear and tear
  • Rheumatoid arthritis: Autoimmune condition
  • Gout: Uric acid crystal buildup
  • Bursitis: Inflamed joint cushions
  • Tendinitis: Inflamed tendons
  • Injuries or repetitive strain: Joint pain from overuse, repeated motion, or physical damage.
  • Lupus: Immune system attacks joints, causing inflammation and pain.
  • Obesity‑related joint stress: Extra weight increases pressure on joints, leading to pain.
  • Age‑related degeneration: Natural wear‑and‑tear of joints over time.
  • Back pain: Muscle strain, herniated discs, etc.

Why Professional Treatment Is the Key to Long‑Lasting Relief

Arthritis and joint pain are complex. Treating them without medical guidance often leads to temporary relief or worsening symptoms. Our team provides the expertise needed to identify the root cause and create a plan that truly works.
