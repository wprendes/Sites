Common Causes of High Cholesterol and Why You Shouldn’t Ignore Them

Cholesterol levels can rise for many reasons, and without proper evaluation, it’s easy to overlook the true cause or miss early warning signs.

Common causes and risk factors for high cholesterol

  • Poor diet: High intake of saturated fats, trans fats, and processed foods.
  • Genetics: Family history of high cholesterol or heart disease.
  • Obesity: Excess weight increases LDL (“bad”) cholesterol.
  • Physical inactivity: Lack of exercise lowers HDL (“good”) cholesterol.
  • Smoking: Damages blood vessels and reduces healthy cholesterol levels.
  • Diabetes: High blood sugar contributes to higher LDL and triglycerides.
  • Age: Cholesterol naturally rises as you get older.
  • Hypothyroidism: Low thyroid function can elevate cholesterol.
  • Liver or kidney conditions: These can affect how your body processes fats.
  • Stress: Chronic stress may influence cholesterol and heart health.

Why Professional Treatment Is Essential for Long‑Term Heart Protection

High cholesterol is more than just a number — it’s a sign that your cardiovascular system needs attention. Without proper medical guidance, cholesterol can continue to rise, increasing your risk of heart attack, stroke, and other serious conditions. Our team provides the expertise needed to identify the root cause and create a plan that truly supports your long‑term heart health.
